# BLOODSTREAM — CONTENT STANDARDS

Status: AUTHORITATIVE QUALITY BAR

## Purpose
Bloodstream is large, but size must never be achieved by flooding the world with interchangeable tasks. Every piece of player-facing content must justify its existence.

## The test
Before content is accepted, ask:
1. What makes this situation specific to Bloodstream?
2. Why does it happen in this exact place?
3. What can the player observe or decide?
4. What human or world truth does it reveal?
5. How is it mechanically or emotionally different from nearby content?

## Major bounty standard
Every major bounty requires:
- a memorable target with motive and relationships;
- at least two information sources;
- an investigation or tracking phase;
- a location with narrative logic;
- at least one complication;
- a resolution that is not just "reduce health to zero";
- persistent aftermath.

## Settlement standard
Every major settlement requires:
- economic reason to exist;
- power structure;
- social classes and work patterns;
- day/night rhythm;
- distinct architecture/material palette;
- surrounding resource network;
- 10+ recurring named locals at full production scale;
- at least one joyful/ordinary space not tied to crime.

## Wilderness standard
Wilderness is allowed to be empty. Do not place collectibles every hundred meters. Discovery should come from ecology, landmarks, traces, weather and occasional authored situations.

## Quest standard
Do not ship repeated templates disguised by names. If two missions share a mechanical skeleton, their social context, information structure, space and consequence must materially differ.

## Reward standard
Rewards may be information, access, trust, a new route, payment, equipment, evidence, changed world state or simply an authored experience. Not every activity needs loot.

## Writing standard
Characters do not know they are quest NPCs. Dialogue must not sound like task assignment UI. Avoid exposition that the speaker would not naturally say.

## Visual standard
No landmark or hero location ships as untouched procedural output. Procedural generation can establish terrain/foliage foundations, but composition, routes, sight lines and human history require authored review.

## AI-generated content policy
Generative tools such as Higgsfield can accelerate concepting, reference, variation and selected production steps. Final assets and writing must be curated, legally usable, coherent with canon and technically validated. AI output is never accepted simply because it exists.

## Completion standard
A feature is not "done" because code or an asset exists. It is done when integrated, tested in the real game flow, performant at target budget and consistent with authority documents.
