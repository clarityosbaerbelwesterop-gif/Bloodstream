# BLOODSTREAM — HISTORICAL & CULTURAL AUTHENTICITY

Status: DOMAIN AUTHORITY

## Purpose
Bloodstream uses a fictional frontier map but aims for historically believable material culture, institutions and human behavior. Authenticity is a source of stronger design, not a museum constraint.

## Era policy
The exact year is RESEARCH-GATED. The desired window is the late post-Civil-War frontier period, before the West feels fully modernized but after rail, telegraph and cartridge firearms are widespread enough to support the game's systems.

Before asset lock, research must validate firearms and ammunition, rail technology, mining practices, law jurisdictions, clothing by class/region, horse equipment, medicine, food preservation, communications, wages, prices and bounty values.

## Indigenous representation
The protagonist's exact nation, language, naming conventions and community practices are not to be invented casually. Final canon requires targeted research and knowledgeable cultural review.

Non-negotiables:
- community members are individuals, not mystical archetypes;
- the community has humor, work, disputes, romance, children, elders and ordinary life;
- no generic mixture of unrelated traditions;
- spirituality is not a magic system;
- sacred practices are not collectibles or combat buffs;
- survivors and living communities continue after the opening;
- identity affects relationships and institutions without reducing every scene to prejudice;
- prejudice, where present, is historically grounded and character-specific.

## Opening violence
The childhood catastrophe must be emotionally devastating without becoming exploitation. Attachment comes before destruction. The child perspective limits what is shown and understood. Victims retain dignity and personality. Sexual violence, if retained, is never interactive, voyeuristic or graphically staged; narrative weight comes through consequence and survivor perspective.

## Fictional geography
The world may compress multiple environmental zones, but transitions must be geologically and ecologically legible. Rail lines, rivers, passes, settlements and economies should explain why towns exist where they do.

## Names and language
Culturally specific names are research-gated. Placeholder labels such as THE HUNTER are allowed internally until validated names are chosen. Avoid caricature dialect spelling; distinguish people through vocabulary, rhythm, syntax and worldview.

## Historical truth vs game clarity
When historical accuracy conflicts with usability, document the deviation rather than silently modernizing the world.

## Required research pack
Before Milestone 5 closes, validate:
1. era and frontier politics;
2. Indigenous nation/language decision;
3. rail and mining economy;
4. firearms and ammunition;
5. wildlife and hunting;
6. medicine, injury and food;
7. law, bounty practice and jurisdiction;
8. architecture and settlement patterns.

No final cultural asset should be produced from AI output alone.
