# BLOODSTREAM — HIGGSFIELD-ASSISTED CONTENT PIPELINE

Status: PRODUCTION PIPELINE STANDARD

## Purpose
Higgsfield may accelerate concept art, mood studies, reference exploration, previs, short cinematic prototypes and selected source imagery/video. It does not replace Unreal integration, technical art, authored design or legal/provenance review.

## Allowed pipeline stages
1. **Design brief** — start from authority docs, region/character requirements and exact deliverable.
2. **Reference board** — historical/photo references and internally generated exploration.
3. **Higgsfield exploration** — controlled prompts for silhouette, lighting, costume/material direction, weather, shot ideas or motion studies.
4. **Selection** — reject generic, inconsistent or historically implausible output.
5. **Human/agent refinement** — paintover, modeling brief, material plan, animation/camera plan or other transformation.
6. **Production asset** — build/import into the actual Unreal pipeline.
7. **Validation** — provenance, visual continuity, scale, performance, collision/rigging/LOD and in-game lighting.
8. **Archive** — store prompt/version/source metadata for reproducibility where practical.

## Best uses
- regional mood exploration;
- settlement visual ideation;
- costume silhouette variants;
- keyframe/previs for authored scenes;
- weather and time-of-day studies;
- marketing concept exploration after canon is stable;
- animation/video reference that will be recreated or integrated through a controlled pipeline.

## Bad uses
- mass-generating final props with no review;
- inventing culturally specific sacred objects or dress without research;
- replacing canonical character design every prompt;
- shipping inconsistent output solely because it is fast;
- generating mission content to inflate quantity;
- treating a rendered video as proof that equivalent real-time Unreal gameplay exists.

## Character consistency
Before large-scale character generation, lock a character sheet: age, proportions, face, hair, scars/marks, clothing layers, color/material notes, region and chapter variants. Store approved reference images and seed/reference strategy where supported.

## Environment consistency
Each region gets a visual bible with geology, vegetation, architecture, road/rail language, weather, palette and forbidden motifs. Prompts must cite that internal brief rather than free-associate Western clichés.

## Cultural safeguard
Any Indigenous visual material remains concept-only until checked against the cultural authenticity process. Generated imagery is never considered a cultural source.

## Unreal handoff
Every accepted concept must end as an actionable UE deliverable: environment blockout, modular kit brief, texture/material set, MetaHuman/character brief, animation reference, Niagara target or Sequencer shot plan.

## Truth rule
Never label generated imagery as an in-engine screenshot or playable build unless it genuinely came from the real project.
