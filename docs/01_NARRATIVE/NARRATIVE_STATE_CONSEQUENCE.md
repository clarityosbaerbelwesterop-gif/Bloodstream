# BLOODSTREAM — NARRATIVE STATE & CONSEQUENCE

Status: SYSTEM/NARRATIVE CONTRACT

## Principle
The world remembers meaningful actions without reducing morality to a visible score.

## State layers

### Immediate state
Who is alive, injured, captured, escaped, hostile, indebted or present.

### Relationship state
Trust, fear, resentment, affection, obligation and professional respect are tracked separately where useful. A person can respect the protagonist and still hate him.

### Settlement state
Law pressure, economic disruption, business availability, rumor, local safety and faction control may change after major events.

### Campaign evidence state
Documents, testimony, ledgers and physical proof can open or close later routes. Evidence should be a real narrative resource.

### Memory state
NPCs remember a curated set of authored facts rather than every microscopic action. Memory must support believable callbacks without exploding complexity.

## Consequence rules
- no universal karma number;
- no "good ending points" UI;
- consequences should appear through people, spaces, prices, rumors, missions and available allies;
- outcomes may be delayed by hours or chapters;
- some choices should remain ambiguous rather than being instantly graded.

## Death permanence
Named recurring NPC death is persistent unless the scene is explicitly non-canonical or reloaded before save commitment. Story logic must not resurrect people through later content.

## Bounty persistence
If a target escapes, that outcome can generate a later encounter. If captured alive, transport/jail/prisoner-transfer consequences may appear. If killed by a rival, the player's information path changes.

## World repair
The game should model limited recovery. A burned building may be rebuilt, a mine may reopen under new ownership, a road may become safer. Consequence does not mean the world stays frozen in damage forever.

## Save commitment
Major irreversible decisions must create clear save-state checkpoints. The technical persistence design must expose stable IDs for characters, missions, settlements, evidence and world events.
