# BLOODSTREAM — CHARACTER BIBLE

Status: WORKING CANON

## Protagonist — internal codename: THE HUNTER
Exact personal name and nation: RESEARCH-GATED.

### Core traits
Observant, patient, dryly funny, loyal after trust is earned, uncomfortable with pity, capable of tenderness with animals and children, excellent at reading terrain and human behavior, frighteningly focused during pursuit.

### Flaws
He confuses motion with purpose, withholds feelings until they become decisions, can dehumanize a target once convinced of guilt, avoids depending on others and fears that peace would mean forgetting the dead.

### Arc
Belonging → displacement → competence → professional hunter → revenge certainty → moral fracture → discovery of systemic responsibility → choice about what to preserve after vengeance.

## Frank "The Judge" Vance
A contract enforcer turned businessman. Calm, persuasive and materially elegant without being aristocratic. He treats violence as logistics.

Strengths: organization, reading weakness, delegation, reputation, political access.
Flaws: believes everyone has a price; underestimates loyalty and memory; cannot understand sacrifice without profit.

Vance should be able to share a room with the protagonist before their final confrontation without the scene automatically becoming combat.

## Vance inner circle — working roles

### The Butcher
Former execution specialist. Personally sadistic, unlike Vance. Operates in northern snow country. Represents violence enjoyed for itself.

### The Ledger
Bookkeeper/logistics chief embedded in mining operations. Rarely pulls a trigger; responsible for payroll, bribes, transport and evidence. Represents bureaucratic guilt.

### The Sheriff
Lawman protected by Vance money. Publicly respectable. Represents legitimacy used as armor.

### The Scout
Tracker who once helped locate vulnerable routes and settlements. Guilty, but no longer loyal to Vance. Designed as a morally complicated target.

### The Rail Captain
Controls armed train security and prisoner transport. Represents industrial force.

## Recurring ally roles
- frontier doctor: competent, exhausted, morally stubborn;
- rival bounty hunter: charismatic, dangerous, sometimes ally, sometimes competitor;
- Lindstripe gunsmith/pawnbroker: first credible firearm path, remembers debts;
- reporter/printer/clerk: connects crimes to documents and institutions;
- survivor A: prioritizes communal survival over vengeance;
- survivor B: mirrors the protagonist's anger;
- survivor C: built another life and resents being pulled back into the past.

## Character rule
Every recurring character requires a desire not centered on the protagonist, a fear, a contradiction, at least one relationship with another NPC, one visible off-mission behavior and an opinion capable of changing through play.

No important character exists only to give missions.
