# BLOODSTREAM — MISSION & DIALOGUE STANDARDS

Status: DESIGN STANDARD

## Mission principle
A Bloodstream mission is a Western situation, not a checklist.

Every major mission must establish:
- what the player wants;
- what each major NPC wants;
- what information is uncertain;
- what changes if the player waits, observes or approaches differently;
- the memorable image, space or human conflict.

## Objective language
Avoid gamey instructions when the world can communicate intent. Prefer "Find where Harrow went" supported by tracks, witnesses and evidence over "Follow fourteen glowing footprints."

## Required mission variety
Across the campaign use pursuits, investigations, social infiltration, escorts with meaningful conversation, defense, live capture, trains, confined-room suspicion, wilderness survival, duels, three-way standoffs, negotiation, rescue, evidence recovery, family/community missions and nonviolent exploration.

## Failure philosophy
Do not fail a mission because the player stepped outside an invisible cinematic corridor. Fail only for meaningful reasons. Where possible, convert mistakes into changed states rather than reload screens.

## Bounty outcomes
Major bounties may support combinations of dead, alive, escaped, surrendered, killed by a rival, killed during chaos, proven innocent/wrong person, turned witness or deliberately released. Not every target needs every outcome.

## Dialogue style
Dialogue is lean, specific and willing to leave meaning unstated.

Rules:
- no constant quips during danger;
- no lore dumps disguised as conversation;
- use silence;
- people interrupt, evade and misunderstand;
- humor comes from personality and circumstance;
- avoid modern therapy vocabulary unless historically plausible;
- avoid cartoon dialect spelling;
- do not explain an emotion the performance already communicates.

## Player dialogue
The protagonist is authored, not blank. Choices select stance or method rather than a new personality. Valid stances include direct, patient, threatening, dry/humorous, compassionate and silent.

Silence is a legitimate authored response.

## Travel dialogue
Companion conversations react to location, recent events and relationship state. They must not endlessly repeat. Important conversations can pause naturally if interrupted and resume later.

## Cinematic handoff
Gameplay enters and exits cinematics with spatial continuity. Do not remove control merely to show actions the player could perform.
