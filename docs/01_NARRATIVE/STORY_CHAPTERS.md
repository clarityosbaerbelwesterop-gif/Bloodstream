# BLOODSTREAM — STORY CHAPTERS

Status: WORKING CAMPAIGN SPINE

This is the authoritative chapter order for pre-production. Individual mission counts remain adjustable.

## Prologue — HOME
Live inside the childhood community. Learn family, tracking, interaction and place. Warning signs precede the Vance-led attack. Escape and displacement end the prologue.

## Chapter 1 — YEARS ON THE ROAD
Playable transitions establish how the protagonist learned to survive and work. Adult gameplay begins with modest hunting, escort and bounty jobs. Competence is earned rather than granted with a complete arsenal.

## Chapter 2 — LINDSTRIPE
The protagonist arrives nearly broke in a rough mining town. He acquires his first dependable revolver through work, debt or trade. A bounty board reveals faces tied to the childhood attack. He also learns he is not the only hunter pursuing them.

## Chapter 3 — THE FIRST NAMES
Several targets teach the complete bounty loop. One is clearly monstrous, one morally complicated, and one knows more than expected. Evidence points toward Vance's modern commercial network.

## Chapter 4 — IRON COUNTRY
Rail corridors, company security and mobile targets open. The protagonist discovers how old violence became modern business. Rival hunter relationships deepen.

## Chapter 5 — WHITE COUNTRY
Northern mountains and deep snow. A pursuit culminates in a confined ensemble sequence where weather traps allies, suspects, a target and strangers together. Information and mistrust matter as much as gunplay.

## Chapter 6 — BLOOD AND LEDGERS
Mine payrolls, land transfers, bribes and account books connect Vance to clients. The protagonist must choose when evidence is worth more than an immediate kill.

## Chapter 7 — SOUTHERN DUST
The dry frontier opens. Competing hunters, smugglers and law create multi-sided conflicts. One campaign-signature three-way standoff belongs here.

## Chapter 8 — THE SURVIVORS
The protagonist reconnects with survivors from his childhood community. They challenge the story he has told himself about what the dead would want.

## Chapter 9 — THE JUDGE
The hunt for Vance becomes direct. Vance uses negotiation, leverage, witnesses and divided loyalties rather than hiding as a simple fortress boss. The confrontation resolves the personal revenge thread but not the larger system.

## Chapter 10 — AFTER THE SHOT
Consequences spread. Removing Vance creates a vacuum and exposes the people who hired, protected and replaced men like him.

## Chapter 11 — THE MACHINE
Final campaign against documentary evidence, protected actors and institutional power. Objectives include witness protection, exposing crimes, defending communities and choosing which alliances are tolerable.

## Epilogue — WHAT REMAINS
A quieter playable epilogue reflects relationships and choices. The final image centers life continuing rather than a pile of bodies.

## Pacing requirements
- Every chapter contains at least one memorable low-violence sequence.
- No chapter is only combat.
- Travel and landscape provide decompression.
- Major deaths require prior relationship investment.
- Vance affects the player long before the final confrontation.
- The chapter structure may branch locally but should preserve a coherent authored arc.
