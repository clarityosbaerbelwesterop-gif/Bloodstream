# BLOODSTREAM — FAUNA & ECOLOGY

Status: DOMAIN AUTHORITY

## Goal
Wildlife should make the frontier feel alive while supporting hunting, danger, observation and ecosystem storytelling.

## Animal categories
- large herbivores: deer/elk/pronghorn/bison equivalents as historically appropriate;
- predators: wolves, cougars, bears or regional equivalents;
- small game: rabbits, squirrels and similar;
- birds: raptors, scavengers, waterfowl, songbirds;
- aquatic life where fishing is implemented;
- domestic animals: horses, cattle, dogs, chickens, mules, pigs, etc.

Final species lists are region and era validated before asset production.

## Behavior
Animals need readable states: feeding, drinking, resting, traveling, alert, fleeing, defending, hunting and caring for young where applicable. Predators do not attack the player on sight as a default game trope.

## Ecology rules
- water and food sources affect placement;
- time of day affects activity;
- weather affects visibility and movement;
- carcasses attract scavengers;
- human activity lowers wildlife density near busy roads/towns;
- overhunting may temporarily reduce local populations but should recover over time.

## Hunting value
Animals provide meat, hides, fat, antler/bone and other historically plausible materials. Avoid arcade loot rarity colors. Quality depends on species, condition and shot placement rather than random drops.

## Tracking clues
Tracks, scat, hair, broken vegetation, blood, calls, feeding signs and disturbed water can all indicate animals. Clues fade according to weather and time.

## Legendary/unique animals
No magical glowing animals. Exceptional specimens may exist as authored natural-history encounters with distinctive location/behavior, not supernatural statistics.

## Animal welfare tone
The game allows hunting because it is part of frontier survival/economy, but does not trivialize wounded animals. Clean kills are mechanically and audiovisually distinct from poor hits.

## Horses
Horse behavior and systems are owned by `HORSES_TRAVEL.md`, but domestic and wild horse ecology must remain consistent with this file.
