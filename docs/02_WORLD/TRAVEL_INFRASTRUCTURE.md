# BLOODSTREAM — TRAVEL & INFRASTRUCTURE

Status: WORLD SYSTEM STANDARD

## Travel hierarchy
1. walking/on-foot exploration;
2. horse/mule travel;
3. wagon/stagecoach in authored/systemic contexts;
4. train travel once rail access is established;
5. limited boat travel in river/wetland regions.

## Roads
Road quality communicates wealth and use. Main roads are faster and socially active but more visible to law/enemies. Trails are slower, quieter and more vulnerable to weather.

## Rail
Rail is both transport and world system. Trains follow schedules within technical limits. Stations create predictable population pulses. Freight, passenger, mail/security and prisoner contexts support missions.

The player may ride trains normally and participate in authored roof/carriage sequences. Freeform train robbery is optional until law, AI and technical simulation prove reliable.

## Stagecoach
Stagecoach travel can serve as contextual fast travel after routes are discovered. It should show a short cinematic transition or real departure/arrival rather than a magical menu teleport.

## Navigation
Default navigation uses physical map, roads, signs, landmarks, sun direction, rail lines and known routes. Accessibility options may add stronger guidance.

## Camps and shelter
Safe rest locations include player camp, inns, rented rooms and selected shelters. Extreme weather can make shelter choice meaningful.

## Infrastructure as history
Bridges, abandoned routes, telegraph poles, water towers, mines and rail cuts visually tell who invested in an area and what they extracted.

## Journey pacing
Long journeys must allow conversation, weather, hunting, observation and camp decisions. The game should never punish players for wanting faster repeat traversal once a route has been meaningfully learned.
