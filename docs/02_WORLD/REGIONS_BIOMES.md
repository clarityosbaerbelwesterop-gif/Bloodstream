# BLOODSTREAM — REGIONS & BIOMES

Status: WORKING WORLD CANON

## 1. Lindstripe Basin
Central early-game territory. Mud, mining dust, pasture, creek valleys and rail activity. Weather changes quickly. The region teaches the game's social and economic systems.

Signature: smoke from mine stacks against wide green-brown valleys.

## 2. Alder Country
Dense mixed woodland, riverbanks, meadows and hunting trails. Rich animal life and excellent tracking terrain. Smaller farms and logging routes create conflict between land use and wilderness.

Signature: shafts of morning light through wet forest and distant elk calls.

## 3. Iron Vale
Steeper industrial country connecting Lindstripe to the mountains. Open pits, deep mines, timber camps, blasting scars, trestles and worker settlements.

Signature: an enormous timber rail bridge crossing a ravine above company housing.

## 4. White Crown Range
High mountains and northern plateau. Snow depth, wind, ice and visibility become major gameplay conditions. Inns, trapping cabins, remote stations and one snowbound ensemble location support suspense.

Signature: sun breaking through a storm over a vast white pass.

## 5. Blackwater Reach
Slow rivers, marsh, cypress-like wetlands where ecologically appropriate, fog, insects, reeds and flooded paths. Boats supplement horses locally. Sound carries strangely through mist.

Signature: lanterns reflected in black water while distant thunder approaches.

## 6. Red Mesa Frontier
Dry high desert and canyon country. Abandoned forts, graveyards, mesas, flash-flood channels and sparse settlements. Long sight lines make cover and water meaningful.

Signature: a rail line cutting across red rock under a huge evening sky.

## 7. Southern Dustlands
Hottest and most exposed region. Border trade, ranch routes, smugglers and competing jurisdictions. Travel timing matters during midday heat.

Signature: dust devils moving across open land as riders appear as tiny silhouettes.

## Transition rules
Biome changes require geographic causes: elevation, river basin, rain shadow, latitude and soil. Never place snow mountain, swamp and desert side-by-side without believable transition space.

## Regional systemic identity
Each region gets distinct:
- weather risks;
- animal roster;
- plant/material resources;
- road quality;
- law pressure;
- dominant industries;
- architecture;
- ambient sound;
- horse/travel considerations;
- bounty archetypes.

## Seasonal philosophy
The full game does not require a real-time four-season simulation. Story chapters and regional climate can shift conditions. Persistent snow line, drought, storm periods and autumn/spring transitions may be authored where they improve narrative and visuals.
