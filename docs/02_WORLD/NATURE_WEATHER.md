# BLOODSTREAM — NATURE & WEATHER

Status: DOMAIN AUTHORITY

## Nature philosophy
Nature is not backdrop. It affects visibility, travel speed, tracking, temperature, sound, fire, animals, NPC schedules and player decisions.

## Weather families
- clear/dry;
- overcast;
- light rain;
- heavy rain/thunderstorm;
- fog/mist;
- wind events;
- dust storm where regionally valid;
- snowfall;
- blizzard/whiteout;
- thaw/freeze transitions at elevation.

Weather transitions should be forecastable through clouds, wind, animal behavior, light and NPC comments rather than purely random switches.

## Gameplay effects
Rain can soften ground, preserve or erase certain tracks, reduce fire reliability and drive civilians indoors. Wind affects smoke, dust, sound direction and selected projectile behavior without becoming a ballistic simulator. Snow records tracks but can bury them. Heat changes animal activity and travel comfort. Fog changes sight lines and tension.

## Extreme weather
Extreme events are rare enough to remain memorable. A blizzard or dust storm can force shelter, alter routes and create authored encounters. Do not trigger disasters constantly.

## Day/night
Lighting and population behavior change strongly across the day. Night is genuinely darker outside settled areas but remains playable through moonlight, lanterns, fire and adaptation/accessibility controls.

## Natural sound
Use layered environmental audio: wind through different vegetation, river scale, insects, distant animals, tree movement, snow absorption, echo in canyons, rain on varied materials. Silence is a designed state.

## Vegetation
Plants communicate moisture, elevation and human land use. Avoid random foliage scatter. Grazed land, burned land, logging, farming and mining damage must visibly differ from unmanaged wilderness.

## Fire
Campfire is core. Larger wildfire simulation is optional and must not be promised until technical validation. Local flammable reactions may exist, but no uncontrolled systemic feature should destabilize missions or performance without strong testing.

## Beauty budget
Every major region requires deliberate scenic compositions and transition reveals. Some rides should exist primarily to let players inhabit the landscape.
