# BLOODSTREAM — AUDIO & MUSIC

Status: DOMAIN AUTHORITY

## Audio thesis
Sound should make distance, weather, interiors and silence tangible. Music supports emotion but does not constantly tell the player what to feel.

## Environmental audio
Regions require distinct wind, vegetation, insects, birds, water, industry, animals and distant human activity. Occlusion and reverb differentiate saloons, cabins, canyons, forests, mines and snow.

## Weapon sound
Major firearms require recognizable mechanical character plus near, mid and far reports. Interior discharge, mountain echo, forest absorption and open-plain reflections should differ.

## Horse and travel sound
Hoof material, tack, saddle, breathing, wagon wheels and rail rhythm are high-priority because they accompany long travel periods.

## Music identity
Create an original score inspired by Western instrumentation and dramatic principles without imitating protected melodies or signature arrangements.

Potential palette after composer exploration: acoustic/electric guitar textures, sparse percussion, harmonica or reed colors, bowed strings and restrained voice. Culturally specific instruments require research and collaboration.

## Leitmotifs
The childhood home/family may have a simple original motif that returns rarely across decades. Vance and institutional power should avoid cartoon-villain scoring; musical treatment can evolve from personal threat into industrial rhythm.

## Silence
Silence is a feature. Scenic overlooks, pre-duel tension, snow and aftermath should sometimes suppress score almost entirely.

## Dynamic score
Music reacts to pursuit, uncertainty, recognition and confrontation rather than merely switching between exploration and combat modes.

## Diegetic music
Saloons, homes, camps, community gatherings and traveling performers can supply local music. Repertoire must match era and culture after research.

## Accessibility
Support independent dialogue, effects and music levels, subtitle speaker labels and directional-sound accessibility options.
