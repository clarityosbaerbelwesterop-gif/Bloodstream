# BLOODSTREAM — ART & ENVIRONMENT DIRECTION

Status: DOMAIN AUTHORITY

## Visual thesis
Beauty and hardship occupy the same frame. Bloodstream should look tactile, weathered and natural rather than permanently brown or desaturated.

## Palette
Palette follows environment and time: warm household firelight, wet greens, mining soot, pale high-country winter, saturated storm skies, sun-bleached southern stone and deep blue night. Avoid a universal Western sepia filter.

## Materials
Prioritize believable wood aging, iron, leather, cloth, mud, dust, snow, rock, glass, painted signs and vegetation. Surfaces tell use: polished rails, worn thresholds, wagon ruts, soot, water stains and hand repair.

## Architecture
Architecture comes from economy, climate, available materials and settlement age. Lindstripe cannot share a kit identity with a southern trade town or mountain waypoint.

## Landscape composition
Design long-distance silhouettes, reveal points and natural framing. Large vistas must also contain navigational information: river, pass, rail, smoke, town lights or recognizable formations.

## Density
Do not clutter wilderness to prove content density. Negative space is part of the Western language.

## Characters
Faces should be age, weather and work specific. Clothing layers show occupation, wealth, climate and repair. Avoid theme-park cleanliness and exaggerated cowboy uniformity.

## Cultural art
Indigenous clothing, objects, architecture and visual motifs require the authenticity process. AI-generated visual output is reference exploration only until reviewed and rebuilt into validated production assets.

## Lighting
Use physically plausible natural light as the baseline with controlled cinematic shaping. Interiors rely on windows, fire, lanterns and practical sources. Night remains legible without looking artificially day-lit.

## Iconic image goal
Every region needs at least five compositions that would identify it instantly with the HUD removed.
