# BLOODSTREAM — BOUNTY HUNTING & INVESTIGATION

Status: DOMAIN AUTHORITY

## Fantasy
A bounty is a human problem to solve, not a waypoint to clear.

## Bounty lifecycle
1. **Acquire** — poster, sheriff, private client, rumor, letter, witness or discovered evidence.
2. **Verify** — understand identity, crime, reliability and whether the contract is legitimate.
3. **Research** — habits, associates, routes, aliases, injuries, preferred places, recent sightings.
4. **Prepare** — weapon, clothing, food, restraints, horse supplies, route.
5. **Track** — physical spoor, witnesses, records, telegraph, campsite evidence, schedules.
6. **Locate** — determine current space and social context.
7. **Approach** — stealth, conversation, ambush, legal arrest, disguise/social entry where authored.
8. **Resolve** — capture, kill, surrender, escape, reveal mistake, recruit as witness, rival intervention.
9. **Deliver/Report** — transport body/prisoner/evidence where appropriate.
10. **Consequence** — payment, rumor, legal reaction, faction response, later callback.

## Information model
Each major target has a dossier composed of facts with confidence levels:
- confirmed;
- credible;
- rumor;
- false/misleading.

The UI must not automatically label every rumor as false. Reliability is learned through sources and contradictions.

## Tracking
No continuous glowing trail by default. Use readable sign: prints, broken brush, blood, camp ash, manure, discarded objects, frightened witnesses, horse-shoe patterns, wagon tracks, weather effects.

An optional accessibility assist may enhance valid clues temporarily without revealing the final answer.

## Social investigation
The player can ask, bribe, intimidate, trade information, observe routines, read notices/ledgers, inspect rooms and follow associates. Witnesses may lie for fear, loyalty, money or misunderstanding.

## Target routines
Major bounty targets have authored state machines and relocation logic. Some remain in a hidden site; others move between safe houses, work, trains or camps.

## Capture alive
Live capture requires meaningful mechanics: surrender states, disarm, restraint, transport, escape risk and law/client acceptance. It cannot be a simple health threshold.

## Rival hunters
Selected bounties may be contested. Rivals can obtain different clues and arrive independently. They should not teleport near the player merely to force drama.

## Signature bounty types
- snowbound identity mystery;
- train passenger under alias;
- mine foreman protected by company law;
- target traveling with family/innocents;
- wrongfully accused person;
- dangerous sadist who must be tracked through wilderness;
- target who wants to surrender but fears the client;
- three-hunter race across Red Mesa;
- escaped prisoner with changing route;
- former Vance member carrying evidence.

## Content target
Aim for 30–50 major handcrafted bounty stories plus smaller procedural/legal jobs only if they meet the same simulation standards. Quality beats count.
