# BLOODSTREAM — HORSES & PLAYER TRAVEL

Status: DOMAIN AUTHORITY

Horseback travel is one of Bloodstream's main experiences. Controls must be responsive, grounded and readable across roads, forests, hills, shallow water and mountain paths.

Individual horses differ modestly in temperament, acceleration, endurance, size and sure-footedness. Familiarity grows through use and care rather than repetitive grinding. Tack, saddle bags and bedrolls define travel utility and carrying capacity.

Animation must support convincing walk, trot, canter and gallop transitions, terrain-aware foot placement, obstacle avoidance and natural reactions to weather, animals and noisy environments.

The horse behaves as a companion in the world rather than a vehicle menu. Calling, following, hitching, feeding, resting and camp interactions should feel physical and consistent. Off-screen catch-up is allowed when needed for pacing, but visible teleporting is not.

Wagons, stagecoaches, trains and boats complement mounted travel rather than replacing it.
