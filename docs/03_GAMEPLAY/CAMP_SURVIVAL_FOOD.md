# BLOODSTREAM — CAMP, SURVIVAL & FOOD

Status: DOMAIN AUTHORITY

## Camp fantasy
The player can stop almost anywhere sensible, make a small camp and turn the wilderness into a temporary home. The camp is intimate, practical and mobile—not a settlement builder.

## Core camp actions
- light and manage a fire where conditions allow;
- cook simple food;
- brew period-appropriate drinks;
- sleep and rest;
- maintain weapons and gear;
- review map, journal, bounties and evidence;
- change clothing and loadout;
- craft limited field items;
- treat minor wounds;
- interact with the horse;
- host occasional authored visitors or companions.

## Camp placement
Terrain, weather, proximity to towns or enemies, water and fire safety influence valid placement. Avoid arbitrary restrictions except where story staging requires them.

## Survival philosophy
Survival creates preparation and atmosphere, not chores. Primary needs are rest/fatigue, hunger over meaningful timescales, temperature/weather exposure, wound state and horse condition.

No meter should demand attention every few minutes.

## Food
Food has preparation, freshness and modest situational benefits without potion-like buffs. A hot meal in cold weather can improve recovery; coffee can reduce fatigue temporarily; preserved food travels well.

Food sources include hunted meat, purchased staples, bread, grains or beans, preserved goods, produce where regionally plausible and meals at inns or homes.

## Cooking
Cooking emphasizes sound, firelight and downtime. Recipes are practical rather than a huge crafting tree. Better ingredients improve recovery and comfort, not combat damage percentages.

## Clothing
Layers and regional outfits influence exposure. Severe environments create meaningful clothing decisions; ordinary weather changes should not force constant outfit management.

## Rest
Sleep advances time and supports recovery. Unsafe camp interruptions are rare and contextual; constant random attacks are forbidden.

## Camp tone
Camp is one of the game's emotional lungs. Rain on canvas, distant wildlife, crackling fire, horse movement and sparse music should make staying still enjoyable.
