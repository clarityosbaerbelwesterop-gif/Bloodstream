# BLOODSTREAM — NPC AI & SOCIAL SIMULATION

Status: DOMAIN AUTHORITY

NPCs should appear to have lives rather than wait for the player. Use three simulation tiers: major authored characters with persistent schedules and relationships; recurring locals with jobs, homes and limited memory; ambient population with role-based routines.

Schedules support work, meals, travel, social time, rest, weather reactions and train/event pulses. Social reactions consider local reputation, visible behavior, trespassing, time, place and relationships.

Conversations are sparse and contextual. Repetition is aggressively controlled. NPCs may refuse, defer or shorten conversation when busy or under pressure.

Groups can gather, disperse, queue, watch events and react to environmental change. Opponents value self-preservation and may warn, negotiate, retreat or surrender when appropriate to role and situation.

Persist only meaningful memories such as help, betrayal, debt, contract outcomes and major local incidents. Distant NPCs use simplified simulation; full perception and animation are reserved for nearby relevance.
